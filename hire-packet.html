Mandatory Reporting Acknowledgment

You are a mandatory reporter

This one is law, not policy. Please read it all the way to the bottom — the button unlocks when you do.

Purpose

The purpose of this form is to ensure all staff understand their legal and ethical obligations as mandatory reporters when working with children and families within Spectrum Squad ABA. Protecting the safety and well-being of our clients is a core responsibility of every employee.

1. Mandatory Reporting Requirements

As an employee of Spectrum Squad ABA, I understand that:

  • I am considered a Mandatory Reporter under Nevada law (NRS 432B).
  • I am legally required to report any suspected abuse, neglect, exploitation, or imminent risk of harm involving any child receiving services with Spectrum Squad.
  • A report must be made as soon as practically possible, but no later than 24 hours from when suspicion arises.
  • Reports must be made to the appropriate agency: Child Protective Services (CPS), or local law enforcement if immediate danger is suspected.

2. What Must Be Reported

I understand that I must report suspected:

  • Physical abuse
  • Sexual abuse or sexual exploitation
  • Emotional or verbal abuse
  • Neglect (including lack of supervision, medical neglect, unsafe home conditions, etc.)
  • Exposure to domestic violence
  • Human trafficking indicators
  • Any observable signs that place a child at risk of serious harm

3. How to Make a Report

I understand that:

  • I must immediately notify the Clinical Director or Program Director (Lexie or Quiana) once a concern has been identified.
  • I must complete a written incident report within the same day.
  • I may be required to call CPS directly and provide factual, objective information.
  • Internal discussions do not replace the legal requirement to report.
  • Failure to report may result in legal consequences, including misdemeanor charges, fines, and potential loss of professional certification.

4. Confidentiality

I understand that:

  • Reports are confidential, and I am not to discuss allegations with anyone outside the reporting chain.
  • I am protected from retaliation when reporting in good faith.
  • I may not share details of reports with other staff, parents, or clients.

5. Spectrum Squad Policy

I acknowledge that Spectrum Squad ABA:

  • Requires all staff to uphold mandatory reporting laws.
  • Will provide support and guidance when a report is required.
  • Will document all concerns, reports, and follow-up actions.
  • May take disciplinary action for failure to report, including termination.

6. Staff Acknowledgment

By signing below, I acknowledge that:

  • I have read and understand the Mandatory Reporting requirements.
  • I agree to comply with Nevada law and Spectrum Squad policies.
  • I understand the legal consequences of failing to report.
  • I had the opportunity to ask questions about reporting procedures.
  • I understand that this acknowledgment will be placed in my employee file.

RBT Continuing Education Policy

Twelve PDUs a year

Effective 01.01.2026. Applies to all Registered Behavior Technicians employed by Spectrum Squad.

Purpose

Spectrum Squad is committed to providing high-quality, ethical, and evidence-based services to the individuals and families we serve. Ongoing professional development ensures that Registered Behavior Technicians maintain clinical competency and remain in compliance with the Behavior Analyst Certification Board (BACB) requirements.

This policy outlines the continuing education expectations necessary for maintaining active RBT certification.

Annual Professional Development Requirement

All RBTs are required to complete a minimum of twelve (12) Professional Development Units (PDUs) per certification cycle (annually), in accordance with BACB certification maintenance requirements. If your renewal occurs right when the new requirement begins, the 12 PDUs will apply to your next renewal cycle, not the one already in progress.

PDUs must:

  • Be behavior-analytic in nature
  • Be relevant to the RBT Task List, Ethics Code, or direct service delivery
  • Be obtained through BACB-compliant training sources

Responsibility for Certification Maintenance

RBTs are individually responsible for:

  • Maintaining an active RBT credential at all times
  • Tracking their certification renewal date
  • Completing all required PDUs prior to their renewal deadline
  • Submitting documentation to Spectrum Squad by the required internal deadline

RBTs may not provide services if their certification has expired.

Documentation Requirements

Proof of completed PDUs must be submitted to the Program Director no later than thirty (30) days prior to the RBT's certification renewal date.

Documentation must include:

  • RBT name
  • Title of training
  • Number of PDUs earned
  • Date of completion
  • Name of training provider

Spectrum Squad reserves the right to verify the validity of all submitted continuing education.

Approved Professional Development Activities

Acceptable PDUs include, but are not limited to:

  • Spectrum Squad in-service and clinical trainings
  • BACB-compliant online or in-person trainings
  • Workshops and conferences
  • Assigned competency-based training modules
  • Supervision-related training activities

All activities must meet BACB standards.

Company-Sponsored Training

Spectrum Squad may provide:

  • Ongoing in-house training opportunities
  • Group continuing education events
  • Access to no-cost or low-cost professional development resources

Participation in required company trainings may count toward the annual PDU requirement.

Failure to Meet Requirements

Failure to maintain an active RBT certification due to incomplete PDUs will result in:

  • Immediate removal from the clinical schedule
  • Inability to provide direct services
  • Unpaid administrative leave until certification is reinstated

Continued non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

Commitment to Professional Growth

Spectrum Squad is dedicated to developing highly skilled clinicians and will support staff through structured training, mentorship, and professional advancement opportunities. Professional development is considered an essential component of clinical excellence and quality client care.

IRS Form 8850

One form we can't redraw

This is the federal Work Opportunity Credit pre-screening form. It has to stay exactly as the IRS published it, so this step is download, fill in, sign, photograph, send back.

  1. Open the PDF and complete page 1 only — your name, social security number, address, and the boxes that apply to you.
  2. Page 2 is ours. Leave it alone; we fill that in.
  3. Sign and date the bottom of page 1.
  4. Photograph or scan the signed page and send it back below.
Completing this form is voluntary — that's the IRS's own wording. It may help Spectrum Squad claim a hiring tax credit, and it does not affect whether you are hired. The form has to reach us on or before the day you're offered a job, which is why we're asking now.
Nevada background waiver

This one needs a real pen

The Civil Name Check waiver is what lets the state run your background check. Nevada will not accept a typed or drawn signature on it, so this one gets printed and signed by hand.

Pen to paper, please. The State of Nevada accepts no other kind of signature on this form. A photo of a hand-signed page is fine — a digital signature is not, and the form will come back rejected.
  1. Print both pages. Page 1 is the instruction sheet; page 2 is the form you fill in.
  2. Print legibly in black or blue ink.
  3. Complete every field marked with an asterisk, and date and initial paragraph 2.
  4. Under "Any Other Name Used", give every last/first/middle combination you have used, hyphens included. If you've never used another name, leave it blank. If you need more room, print another copy of page 2.
  5. Sign it by hand. Leave the "For Official Use Only" box empty — that one is not yours to fill in.
  6. Photograph or scan the signed page and send it back below.
